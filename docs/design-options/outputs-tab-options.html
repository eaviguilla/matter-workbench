Outputs tab, five layouts

Every option keeps the Ledger palette and system type the app already uses, and uses plain controls (tabs, tables, pills, radios). They differ in how the screen is divided, what leads, and how dense it is. All five show the same moment: Nadia Kowalczyk's probate call, the Clio Note, the Overview, the Clio form note and the Probate Intake Form with one follow-up shut and two Yes or No answers still empty. Names and data are synthetic.

The form is the page. Follow-ups sit indented under their question with a thin connector, the way they appear on the Clio page. A sticky panel on the right says what is left before the answers go to Clio. The client list collapses into a switcher in the top bar, which frees 190px.
