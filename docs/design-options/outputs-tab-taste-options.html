Outputs tab, five more layouts from the taste skill

These five come from the taste skill's app-screen run, not from impeccable. Each one is a different dealt style with its own tokens, type, shell and one idea taken from this product. They all show the same moment as the first sheet, with the same synthetic data. Nadia Kowalczyk's probate call. Five outputs are on the page. The Probate Intake Form is at 13 of 15 answered, 2 not discussed, and 1 follow-up that Clio does not show until answer 13 is Yes. 1 Tinted frame

The sidebar and top bar are one tinted L. The work sits on a white sheet with a hairline edge and soft corners. One output per tab, the form as a plain list. The tint is a paper stone, taken from the product's world. This is the closest to what the app does today, only tidier.

2 Ledger

No sidebar. The client and call are two selects in the top bar, which frees the full width. Left, a ledger of the call's outputs with their versions and counts. How these were drawn. Styles dealt by draw.mjs --app for kind builder and set one per option: framed-sheet, achromatic-ledger, accent-ladder, editorial-greeting and data-terminal. The shell, tokens and type of each are the dealt values. Every count is computed from the same sixteen entries. The "closest lines" in option 5 and the "why" lines in option 3 are mock text, the app does not produce them today.

Where these depart from the style sheets. The mono face is used for client IDs only. The 22px panels and pill controls in option 4 are the dealt soft style. Option 4's heading is 32px, under the dealt 48px, because the inspector warns above 34px for working screens. Option 3 uses a deep green rather than the dealt raspberry.
